# Net Throttle – Your Ultimate Network Monitor & Bandwidth Controller

**Net Throttle** is an intuitive and feature-rich application that puts you in complete control of your internet traffic on Windows. Whether you want to see what your machine is doing, cap bandwidth-hungry apps, or block unwanted connections, this app gives you real-time visibility and instant control over every process, PID, and connection — all from one clean interface.

---

## Key Features

### Real-Time Network Monitoring
- Live download and upload rates for every process, PID, and individual connection
- Powered by ETW for accurate, low-overhead statistics
- No per-app configuration required — traffic is tracked automatically

### Hierarchical Process Tree
- Processes expand down to their PIDs and individual network connections
- Search the tree to instantly locate any app or connection
- Filters: **All**, **Online**, **Offline**, and **Hidden**

### Bandwidth Limiter
- Set download and upload speed limits per process or per single connection
- Rules are applied instantly and enforced by the kernel driver
- Independent DL and UL limits with their own enabled/disabled toggles

### Traffic Blocker
- Instantly block connections per process or per connection
- Stop unwanted traffic and give priority to the apps you care about
- Master **Blocker** switch right on the toolbar

### Precise Rule Scoping
- Rules can target a whole process, a specific PID, or a specific connection by local/remote address
- Add, edit, and delete rules from the Rules panel
- Rules are saved to disk and reapplied automatically on the next launch

### Ports Filter
- Narrow a process's rules down to specific ports
- Limits and blocks only affect the traffic you actually care about

### Global Hotkeys
- Assign **Hold** or **Toggle** hotkeys to any rule
- Enable or disable rules from anywhere in Windows — no need to switch to the app first
- Support for key combinations (up to 4 simultaneous keys)
- Distinguishes between Left and Right modifiers (e.g. LeftCtrl ≠ RightCtrl)

### Cleaner Process Tracker
- Vanished processes are automatically kept out of the list
- Deleted processes stay hidden until they show network traffic again

### User-Friendly Interface
- Clean and intuitive design
- Easy-to-use controls with system tray integration
- Two built-in themes: **Dark Gray** and **White** — applied instantly at runtime

### Persistent Layout
- Fully resizable process tree columns and info panel
- Your layout is saved and restored across restarts

### System Integration
- Auto-start with Windows
- Run minimized to tray on startup
- Minimize to tray and close to tray functionality
- Single-instance enforcement to prevent duplicate instances
- System tray icon with right-click context menu
